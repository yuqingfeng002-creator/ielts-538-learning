📖 万能主题短文 · 逐句点读

In today’s rapidly changing world, people face new challenges and opportunities.

在当今快速变化的世界，人们面临新的挑战与机遇。

Education plays a significant role in personal development because it enables people to obtain knowledge and overcome difficulties.

教育在个人发展中发挥重要作用，因为它使人们获得知识并克服困难。

However, a lack of resources can limit opportunities, particularly for students from disadvantaged backgrounds.

然而，资源不足可能限制机会，尤其对弱势背景的学生而言。

Technology has the potential to extend access to education, but excessive screen time can distract students from learning.

科技有潜力扩大教育机会，但过多屏幕时间会分散学生学习的注意力。

It is therefore essential to strike a balance between digital convenience and independent thinking.

因此，在数字便利与独立思考之间取得平衡至关重要。

On a broader level, governments have a responsibility to distribute resources fairly and monitor progress.

从更广泛的角度看，政府有责任公平分配资源并监测进展。

Ultimately, meaningful progress does not necessarily require dramatic changes; consistent effort can make a considerable difference.

归根结底，有意义的进步不一定需要剧烈改变；持续努力可以带来显著差异。

⚠️ 原书替换纠错

criminal ≠ conviction；interference ≠ interdependence；bacteria ≠ virus；inappropriate ≠ hard；gene ≠ factor。不要把它们当作完全同义词。
